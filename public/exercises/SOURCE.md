# Exercise image attribution

The exercise illustrations in this directory are sourced from
[yuhonas/free-exercise-db](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), distributed
under [The Unlicense](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/blob/main/LICENSE.md).

The 62 available catalog exercise images are local side-by-side compositions
of each source exercise's `0.jpg` starting position and `1.jpg` finishing
position. `manifest.json` records the exact source ID, local path and reviewed
PNG SHA-256 for every available exercise, plus explicit unavailable decisions
for the other 10 catalog exercises. Source URLs are
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db/main/exercises/<sourceId>/<stage>.jpg`.

See `docs/EXERCISE_IMAGE_AUDIT.md` in the repository for the per-exercise audit,
review limitations and maintenance procedure. Available does not certify
technique or safety. Unavailable exercises intentionally have no picture;
do not substitute an image of different equipment or movement.

Program/day cover art is outside this exercise-photo audit.
